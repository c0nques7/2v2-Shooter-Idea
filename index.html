Zone Arena2v2 attack / defense · prototype

Attackers push across five zones to arm a charge at site A or B. When you go down, a teammate can revive you, or you respawn in a zone you pick. Every return costs you depending on where you come back. Cover breaks under fire: it drops to half height, then to rubble.
